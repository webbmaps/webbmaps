About Me

Graduated in 2025 with a Bachelors degree in Natural Science where i double majored in geography and gis with a minor in geospatial intelligence, while also earning my FAA part 107. Since graduating i have continuud to sharpen and hone my skillset with MOOCs, ESRI training course, and open source certificate courses. These included python (specifically for GIS programming), web development, remote sensing indexes, and UAS photogrametry. For me, everyday is a chance to gain knowledge. I still make maps in my free time just because I can and I am constantly fascinated by our planet, what happens within its terrestial realm, and the effects of anthropogenic cause.
