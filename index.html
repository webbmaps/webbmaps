About Me

Graduated in 2025 with a Bachelor's degree in Natural Science where I double majored in Geography and GIS with a minor in Geospatial Intelligence, while also earning my FAA Part 107. Since graduating I have continued to sharpen and hone my skillset with MOOCs, Esri training courses, and open source certificate courses. These included Python (specifically for GIS programming), web development, remote sensing indexes, and UAS photogrammetry. For me, every day is a chance to gain knowledge. I still make maps in my free time just because I can and I am constantly fascinated by our planet, what happens within its terrestrial realm, and the effects of anthropogenic cause.
